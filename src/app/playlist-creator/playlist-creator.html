HEARTBEAT CHECKLIST

Playlist Rules

Your playlist must follow these rules before generation.

  • Focus setup: 1–2 songs, with albums optional.
  • One focus song: maximum 12 plays.
  • Two focus songs: maximum 10 plays per song.
  • Supported combinations include 2 songs + 1 album and 1 song + 2 albums.
  • Exactly 2 opening and 2 closing buffer tracks.
  • Buffer tracks must be non-focus and non-charting.
  • Exactly 2 low-stream tracks per playlist.
  • Normally 3–4 BTS fillers; 5 only rarely.
  • Exception: 2 fillers only when both exceed 4 minutes and total 8–10 minutes.
  • Only 2–3 non-BTS/local tracks.
  • Repeated focus tracks should use different verified versions when available.
  • Automatic fillers should be unique across generations whenever possible.
  • Track order should follow the reference playlist pattern, with variation each time.
